# Search strategy evaluation

40 in-scope questions, `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, live Atlas queries (generated by `uv run code-assistant eval search --facts`). The LLM sees the top 6. From most lenient to strictest:

- **Hit@6:** at least one relevant code unit is in the top 6.
- **Evidence@6:** share of the labelled evidence lines (`evidence` in `eval/questions.toml`) in the top 6; Full evidence counts the questions where all of it is.
- **Facts@6:** share of the key facts that the top 6 shows, judged by `nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b:free` from the sources alone and backed by a quote that must be in them; All facts counts the questions where every key fact is (the answer is fully there).
- **Latency:** median search time per question, including the query embedding on CPU and, for the reranker, the OpenRouter call.

Vector only: 4 facts the judge called available were rejected because its quote isn't in the sources.

⚠ Hybrid (vector + keyword, RRF): the judge failed on 1 questions, which are left out of Facts.

Hybrid (vector + keyword, RRF): 2 facts the judge called available were rejected because its quote isn't in the sources.

Reranked (nvidia/llama-nemotron-rerank-vl-1b-v2:free): 5 facts the judge called available were rejected because its quote isn't in the sources.
